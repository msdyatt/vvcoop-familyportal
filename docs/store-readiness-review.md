# Store readiness: privacy policy, terms, and what the stores will ask

Reviewed 6 Nov 2026 against the code as it stands. This is an engineering review of
what the app actually does versus what the pages say. It is not legal advice; the items
marked **attorney** need a lawyer's wording.

## Verdict
Not ready to submit. Four things would get the app rejected or the listing pulled, and
none of them is hard. The app itself is in good shape for a store review: invitation-only,
role-based access, no ads, no tracking, no selling of data, passkey sign-in.

## Must fix before submitting
1. **The policy pages say "working draft".** Both `/privacy` and `/terms` carry a banner
   saying they are not a finished legal document, "Last updated: to be set once reviewed",
   and bracketed `[Flag for legal review]` notes. Apple and Google both read the policy
   page; placeholder text is a rejection. **attorney**
2. **"Delete my account" does not delete.** It sets `profiles.status = 'removed'` and signs
   you out; nothing is erased, and the screen says so ("records stay intact... an
   administrator can restore your access"). Apple (5.1.1(v)) and Google Play both require
   account deletion that actually deletes the account and its personal data, started from
   inside the app. Google also wants a public web page where someone can request deletion.
   Needs: a real delete path (auth user, profile, push devices, authored content
   anonymized), a stated rule for what happens to a household's children's records, and a
   `/delete-account` page. Retention of records the co-op must keep is allowed if stated.
3. **No retention policy.** The policy has a bracketed placeholder where "how long we keep
   it" should be. Decide: how long after a family leaves, and who can ask for deletion. **attorney**
4. **The policy omits services that touch family data.** Missing today:
   - **Resend** sends invitation and reminder emails (sees names and email addresses).
   - **Push notifications**: a device token is stored per phone; Apple's push service and
     Google Firebase Cloud Messaging carry the notification text.
   - **Cloudflare Web Analytics**: its script loads on the site (seen in the browser
     console). Confirm in the Cloudflare dashboard whether it is on; if so, disclose it.
   - **Google Fonts**: every page load fetches fonts from Google, which tells Google the
     visitor's IP. Either disclose it or host the fonts ourselves (small change, better).
   - **Error reports**: client errors are logged with page URL and browser details.
   - Supabase, Cloudflare and OpenSign are listed and correct.

## Should fix
5. **Contact address** is `veritasvillagecoop@gmail.com`. Use `admin@veritasvillage.org`
   (or a `support@`) on both pages and in both store listings; the Apple seller is an
   individual account, so a co-op address keeps your personal details off the listing.
6. **Who operates the app.** Name the legal entity (Veritas Village, a 501(c)(3)) as the
   operator in the policy, since the App Store will show an individual as seller.
7. **Children's information section** is a placeholder. The facts, for a lawyer: children
   never sign in or enter their own data; a parent enters name, birthdate or grade, and an
   optional photo; photos are subject to a consent record; teachers may add notes visible
   to the family. Whether COPPA or Texas law applies to a parent-run, invitation-only
   co-op tool is a legal question. **attorney**
8. **Terms** cover using the software but lack the usual sections: governing law (Texas),
   disclaimers and limits of liability, termination, acceptable use, contact. Also needs a
   link to the co-op's own enrollment, waiver and conduct documents rather than restating them. **attorney**
9. **Link both pages from inside the app** (Account Settings and the sign-in page), not only
   the website footer. Both stores prefer it, and it is one line each.
10. **A reviewer demo account.** The app is invitation-only, so Apple and Google need a
    working login (a dummy household with sample data) in the review notes.
11. **A support page.** Apple requires a support URL; a page with the co-op email is enough.

## Already fine
- Privacy and terms pages are public (both return 200, no login), which both stores require.
- Access is by role and household; teachers only see their classes; curriculum files are
  teaching-team only.
- Private calendar links can be regenerated by the user.
- No third-party sign-in, so Sign in with Apple is not required.
- Traffic is HTTPS only, so export-compliance is a one-line answer.
- Dues: the app records status and does not process payments. If Stripe dues (PR #10)
  ever ship, the policy, terms and store privacy answers change, and the payment
  processor must be named.

## Draft answers for the store privacy forms
Data is linked to the user's account; **no tracking** (no advertising or cross-app SDKs).

| Data | Apple label | Google Data safety | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone | Contact Info | Personal info | App functionality |
| Emergency contact (a third party's name and phone) | Contact Info | Personal info | App functionality |
| Child name, birthdate or grade | Other User Content | Personal info | App functionality |
| Child and profile photos | Photos or Videos | Photos and videos | App functionality |
| Teacher notes | Other User Content | Other user-generated content | App functionality |
| Account ID | Identifiers: User ID | Device or other IDs | App functionality |
| Push token | Identifiers: Device ID (declare to be safe) | Device or other IDs | Notifications |
| Error reports | Diagnostics | App info and performance | App functionality |
| Page views (only if Cloudflare Analytics is on) | Usage Data | App activity | Analytics |

Encrypted in transit: yes. Users can request deletion: yes, once item 2 is built.
Not collected: location, contacts, financial details, health, browsing history.
Age rating today: 4+ on Apple. Chat or any user-to-user messaging raises it and brings
extra requirements (below). Not a Kids Category app: it is for parents and teachers.

## Submission checklist
Privacy URL `https://family.veritasvillage.org/privacy` (live), support URL, a demo login,
screenshots (iPhone 6.9-inch and Android phone), description and category (Education),
copyright holder, account-deletion path, push entitlement, the two privacy forms above.

## If chat or messaging is added
Anything that lets users post to each other is "user-generated content". Apple (1.2) and
Google then require: a way to report content, a way to block a user, filtering or
moderation of objectionable content, and published contact details. The terms need an
acceptable-use section, and the age rating rises. All doable; scope it in from the start.
